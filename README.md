# Opal Health Care Case Study 📋

## 📌 Overview

This project was developed as part of the **INFO6050 - Business Analysis** course and focuses on analyzing **Opal Health Care**, a leading provider of residential aged care in Australia. The study explores Opal’s potential transition from provided care to a **self-managed care system**, with a spotlight on **fall prevention and health reporting** among elderly residents.

## 👩‍⚕️ Project Focus

- Analyzed the **fall management protocol** and proposed digital transformation strategies.
- Proposed the integration of **wearable accelerometer sensors** and **telemonitoring ECG devices**.
- Evaluated **organizational readiness** through stakeholder analysis and strategic tools like **PESTLE** and **SWOT**.

## 🔍 Key Objectives

- Identify inefficiencies in current fall detection and reporting systems.
- Model AS-IS vs TO-BE processes using activity and use case diagrams.
- Recommend steps to transition to a **more autonomous, tech-enhanced care model**.
- Perform comprehensive **stakeholder engagement** using a Power/Interest matrix.
- Suggest educational and operational reforms to improve care staff risk awareness.

## 📊 Strategic Tools Used

- **PESTLE Analysis** – Political, Economic, Social, Technological, Legal, Environmental factors.
- **SWOT Analysis** – Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats.
- **Stakeholder Matrix & Strategy** – Tailored engagement strategies based on influence/interest levels.
- **Use Case Diagrams** – Focused on proposed digital fall detection/reporting protocols.

## 🛠 Business Process Modelling

- **Rich Picture** – Illustrated the complexity of the current fall management situation.
- **Activity Diagrams** – Mapped the current and proposed workflows.
- **Use Case Diagrams** – Defined digital interaction points for users, staff, and technology systems.

## ✅ Recommendations

- Deploy **wearable fall detection sensors** linked via Bluetooth to notify staff and caregivers in real-time.
- Replace manual incident reporting with **automated digital reporting tools** to reduce response times and errors.
- Educate care staff on **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)** monitoring to proactively prevent fall risks.
- Conduct **workshops and training** to enhance digital literacy among staff and caregivers.
- Collaborate with **external tech and education partners** to implement wearable device systems.
